THE PROBLEM

Your product works.
But do users know what to do?

You know exactly how your product is supposed to work. Your users don't. The smallest friction can be hard to see when you're the one who built it.

01 / THE UNSEEN DETOUR

They click somewhere you didn't expect.

A button that feels obvious to you might be invisible or mean something else to a first-time visitor.

02 / THE MISSING EXPLANATION

You know they left. Not why.

Completion numbers reveal the outcome. A short, contextual interview can help you understand what they expected.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before your first test?

Do I need to know how to code?

You need to add a small tracking script to a compatible website. If you used an AI coding tool to build your site, it can help you install the snippet.

Will you find five testers for me?

No. You invite up to five people to try your task. First Five provides the testing, feedback, and research workflow.

What can I test?

One defined user journey on a compatible website or web application—for example onboarding, feature discovery, or a simulated checkout.

What happens if someone doesn't finish?

You'll be able to review the supported interactions captured during their attempt. If they complete the feedback questions, you'll also have their explanation. We won't invent a reason when feedback is missing.

Is ₹99 a subscription?

No. It's one one-time purchase for one study with up to five eligible participant attempts and one consolidated AI research report.

When will I receive the report?

After you close the study and the included attempts have been processed. You'll be able to view basic task outcomes earlier when data is available.
